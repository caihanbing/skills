# Field policy

## Priorities

- **P0 feasibility:** total cost ceiling, upfront-cash ceiling, commute gate, room/occupancy, move-in/lease timing, location, and user-stated pet, cooking, registration, accessibility, parking, or exclusion rules.
- **P1 decision-critical:** rent and mandatory fees, utility tariffs, internet, deposit/prepay/agency charges, contract counterparty and exit terms, availability, maintenance/security, renter themes, freshness and conflicts.
- **P2 enrichment:** age, elevator, amenity, orientation, daylight, furnishing and preferences that do not determine feasibility.

Known P0 mismatches remove an Offer. A stated P0 with no evidence is a blocking unknown; an undisclosed user preference is not a fabricated constraint.

## Costs and confidence

Report **known fixed monthly cost**, **variable monthly cost**, **estimated range** (only with method and source), and **unknown recurring cost** separately. Show upfront cash separately; never turn an unknown into zero.

Evidence confidence: **high** needs confirmed relevant P0s, current independent evidence, no material unresolved conflict, and no sole high-risk support. **Medium** has P0 confirmation but limited P1 coverage, independence, or specificity. **Low** applies to blocking P0 unknowns, current price/major fee/contract unknowns, material conflicts, or a key conclusion relying on one high-risk source.

Fit, confidence, and source-level risk stay separate. Do not create a project-level source-risk score.
